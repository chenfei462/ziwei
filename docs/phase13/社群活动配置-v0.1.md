# Phase 13 Community Activity Config v0.1

## Activities

- `challenge_7_day_stargarden`: seven daily low-risk observations.
- `challenge_seasonal_theme`: seasonal or festival reflection cards.
- `challenge_star_atlas_collection`: collect source-linked atlas cards.

## Rules

- Activities are limited to cultural entertainment and self-observation.
- Share cards hide full birth date, birth time, location detail, and camera data.
- No free-form posting, private messaging, or user-generated prediction threads are included in this phase.
- Activity copy must avoid concrete event forecasts and major decision promises.
