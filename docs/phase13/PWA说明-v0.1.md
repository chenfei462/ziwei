# Phase 13 PWA Mobile Version v0.1

## Scope

- `start_url`: `/phase13`
- `offline_fallback`: `/offline`
- `display`: `standalone`
- `theme_color`: `#42b8a8`
- `background_color`: `#111312`

## Mobile Paths

- `/phase13`: mobile growth home with streak, share, referral, and challenge controls.
- `/share/{id}`: privacy-safe share landing snapshot.
- `/offline`: static fallback for unavailable network.

## Privacy Defaults

- Do not expose full birth date.
- Do not expose birth time.
- Do not expose location detail.
- Do not collect camera frames.
- Do not store full birth profiles in growth or referral logs.
