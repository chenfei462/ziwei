# Phase 13 Growth Data Report v0.1

## Metrics

- Daily streak read count.
- Share landing creation count.
- Share opened and invite visit referral events.
- Daily check-in conversion.
- Star atlas unlock behavior.
- Challenge joined and challenge check-in progress.

## Data Boundary

Growth events record behavior type, share snapshot ID, visitor ID when provided, and coarse metadata. They do not record full birth date, birth time, location detail, camera frames, or background images.

## Review Result

The v0.1 implementation supports PWA entry, share landing snapshots, referral tracking, and three bounded challenge templates. Next review should compare mobile revisit, share open, invite conversion, and challenge completion rates.
